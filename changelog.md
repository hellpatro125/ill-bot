# Changelog

## v1.0.0
- Initial release of ILLBot.
- Silicate-style GUI with dark ruby acrylic theme.
- Independent P1 and P2 Autoclickers with adjustable CPS (1-2000).
- High-precision TPS Changer and Speedhack.
- Replay Macro recording and playback engine.
- Trajectory Visualizer with obstacle collision & hazard death boxes.
- Hitbox Visualizer for players and level obstacles.
